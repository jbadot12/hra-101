A smaller first step

The HRA offers a useful action that requires less commitment than scheduling: answer a few questions and receive relevant guidance.

What it is

A guided health assessment

A short, mobile-first experience that uses clinically reviewed questions to provide personalized education and an appropriate next step.

What it is not

A quiz that ends with a score

  • It does not diagnose a condition.
  • It should not end when the answers are collected.
  • It does not replace the health system's care or clinical judgment.

04 · Clinical framework

Every HRA is grounded in a clinical framework

The experience is designed to help a consumer understand an appropriate next step before diagnosis—not to make the diagnosis itself.

LearnWhat to understand: Clinical rigor shapes the questions, result logic, boundaries, and next-step guidance.Recommended stop

Define the intended use

Clarify the population, health topic, consumer need, and care decision the experience is meant to support.

Use clinically reviewed questions

Questions and answer choices are structured to capture relevant context clearly and consistently.

Apply pre-diagnostic logic

Responses shape education and recommendation pathways without presenting the result as a clinical diagnosis.

Guide the next step

The result can point toward education, screening, a provider, a program, or urgent support based on the framework.

!
The clinical boundary

An HRA is pre-diagnostic and does not replace evaluation by a qualified clinician. The ultimate next step for an individual's care requires clinical judgment, especially when symptoms are new, severe, or urgent.

How is an HRA different from a quiz?

A quiz usually collects answers and returns a score or generic result. An HRA connects clinically guided questions to personalized education, an appropriate CTA, consented follow-up, and measurement.

Does an HRA replace a landing page?

No. A landing page builds context and trust. The HRA adds an interactive step that helps the consumer understand what to do next. Specific campaigns may link directly to an HRA, while broader campaigns may use a landing or selection page first.

Does every HRA produce a risk score?

No. Experiences vary. Some use risk bands, while others return screening guidance or another recommended next step. Universal HRA language should focus on clinically guided personalization rather than promising a numerical score.

What determines whether an HRA performs?
  • A clear service-line goal and defined target audience
  • Strong message matching from channel to assessment
  • Useful, verified CTAs and clear operational ownership
  • Consented nurture and appropriate CRM routing
  • Measurement beyond starts and completions
  • Ongoing review and optimization after launch
